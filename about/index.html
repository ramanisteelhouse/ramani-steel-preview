Established 1974

53 years in metals

India's leading supplier and stockist of Nickel & Nickel-based Alloys, Stainless Steel, Titanium & Titanium Alloys, Duplex, Super Duplex and High-Performance Stainless Steel materials, and manufacturer of nickel strips and busbars.

Ramani Steel House is a family-owned partnership firm based in Mumbai. We have worked in metals since 1974, importing and stockholding nickel and nickel-based alloys, stainless steel, titanium and titanium alloys, duplex, super duplex and high-performance stainless steel.

Alongside stockholding, we manufacture nickel, copper, aluminium, nickel-plated steel, nickel-plated copper, clad and bi-metal busbars, primarily for lithium-ion battery manufacturers serving electric vehicle, solar and energy storage applications. We also produce precision sheet metal components, supporting customers from prototype work on laser and waterjet cutting through to volume production by stamping.

Partnership

Nippon Steel Stainless Corporation, Japan

On 8 June 2023, Nippon Steel Stainless Corporation (NSSC) issued a letter of intent to collaborate with Ramani Steel House on stockholding, distribution and sales, and to jointly promote and expand the business of its stainless steel and nickel alloy plates.

This partnership covers hot-rolled plates in the stainless steel and nickel alloy series. Our other product lines are sourced through our wider global mill network.
