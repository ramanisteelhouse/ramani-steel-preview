Aerospace titanium and nickel alloys

Titanium, nickel super alloys and precipitation-hardening stainless for airframe, engine and fastener applications.

Aerospace material is bought on strength-to-weight, high-temperature performance and, above all, traceability. Ramani Steel House is certified to AS9120:2018, the quality management standard written specifically for aerospace stockist distributors.

Grades commonly specified for Aerospace

The final choice always depends on the service conditions. Each grade links to its full specification.

  • Titanium Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V)R56400Airframe structures, engine components and fasteners where strength-to-weight decides.
  • Titanium Grade 9R56320Cold-formed aerospace components, where cold formability matters.
  • Inconel 718N07718Gas turbine discs, shafts, casings and high-strength fasteners.
  • Inconel 625N06625Exhaust systems and ducting exposed to heat and corrosion.
  • Hastelloy XN06002Combustors, transition ducts and afterburner components.
  • Inconel X-750N07750Springs and bolting that must hold their load at temperature.
  • 15-5PHS15500Fittings and precision shafts that need transverse toughness through larger sections.
  • 17-4PH (SUS 630)S17400High-strength brackets, shafts and fasteners where cost matters.
