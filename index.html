Industries

Who we supply

Material selection changes entirely with the service environment. These are the sectors we supply most often.

Aerospace

Titanium, nickel super alloys and precipitation-hardening stainless for airframe, engine and fastener applications.

Oil & Gas

Duplex, super duplex, 6Mo and nickel alloys for downhole, offshore and sour-service conditions.

Chemical

Corrosion-resistant alloys for reactors, heat exchangers and piping in acid and chloride service.

Pharmaceutical

Hygienic-grade stainless steel and nickel alloys for process vessels, piping and clean-in-place systems.

Automobile

Stainless steel, nickel strip and precision components for vehicle systems and battery assemblies.

EV Battery

Nickel strip, busbars and clad conductors for lithium-ion cell interconnection in EV, solar and energy storage packs.

Construction

Structural and architectural stainless steel for building, infrastructure and cladding applications.

Electrical

Pure nickel, copper and plated conductors for electrical connection, contact and current-carrying components.

Questions

Frequently asked

What materials does Ramani Steel House stock?
Pure nickel, nickel alloys and super alloys (Inconel, Incoloy, Monel, Hastelloy and Nimonic), titanium and titanium alloys from Grade 1 to Grade 23, 300-series stainless steel, duplex and super duplex, 6Mo super-austenitic and special stainless grades. We also manufacture nickel, copper, aluminium and clad busbars.
Which forms are these materials available in?
We supply sheets, plates, coil, round bars, flat bars, pipes, tubes, wires, precision strip coils. Availability varies by grade, so send your requirement and we will confirm what is in stock.
Which industries does Ramani Steel House supply?
Aerospace, oil and gas, chemical, pharmaceutical, automobile, EV battery, construction and electrical industries.
Where is Ramani Steel House located?
Our office is at 65/73, C.P. Tank Road, Mumbai 400004, Maharashtra, India. We have supplied customers in India and internationally since 1974.
Can Ramani Steel House cut or process material to size?
Yes. Our in-house processing covers stamping, punching, laser cutting, waterjet cutting, slitting, shearing, band saw cutting. We also produce precision sheet metal components, from prototypes on laser and waterjet through to volume production by stamping.
