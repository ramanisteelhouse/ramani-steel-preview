Nickel 201

Nickel 201 (UNS N02201) to ASTM B162 from Ramani Steel House, importer and stockholder in Mumbai since 1974. Request current sizes, stock and pricing.

Nickel 201 is a commercially pure nickel, UNS N02201 / Werkstoff 2.4068. It is supplied to ASTM B162 in round bar, plate and sheet. The standard sets a minimum tensile strength of 345 MPa and a minimum yield strength of 83 MPa in the annealed condition. Ramani Steel House supplies it from Mumbai, India — from stock and against order, cut to size, with the mill test certificate for the heat supplied.

The low-carbon version of commercially pure nickel. Holding carbon to 0.02% stops graphite forming at the grain boundaries at elevated temperature, so Nickel 201 keeps its ductility in service above about 315 °C where Nickel 200 would embrittle.

Nickel 201 bar surface finishes

Round bar is quoted by finish as well as by diameter, and the finish changes both the surface and how closely the diameter is held. These are the standard designations — tell us which you need and we will confirm what is available in this grade and size.

CodeFinishHow it is producedWhat you get
HFHot finishedHot rolled or forged, then straightened.Dark, scaled surface with the widest diameter tolerance. The usual choice where the bar will be machined all over.
HRAPHot rolled, annealed and pickledHot finished, then annealed and pickled to remove mill scale.Clean matt grey surface with the scale chemically removed, so corrosion resistance is restored. Tolerance as hot finished.
STSmooth turned (peeled)Hot finished bar turned or peeled all over on a machine.Bright machined surface, decarburised and scaled layer removed, closer and more consistent diameter than hot finished.
PRPeeled and reeledPeeled, then reeled (roller straightened and burnished).Peeled surface improved by reeling, with better straightness — specified where the bar feeds automatic machines.

Nickel 201 — frequently asked

Can Nickel 201 be used where Nickel 200 is specified?
Chemically, generally yes — it is the same material with less carbon. Mechanically it is slightly softer: its annealed minimum yield strength is lower, as the table on this page shows. Where the design depends on Nickel 200's strength at ambient temperature, check that margin before substituting.
