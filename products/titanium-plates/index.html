What is plate?

Titanium plate is the heavier flat-rolled product: hot rolled, annealed and descaled, supplied flat in cut sizes. It is used for pressure equipment, heat-exchanger tube sheets, structural parts and components machined from the solid.

Plate is covered by the same specification as sheet — ASTM B265, which spans titanium and titanium alloy strip, sheet and plate — with ASME SB-265 as its pressure-vessel form. Grade 2 is the commercially pure grade most widely available in plate; Grade 5 is chosen where strength decides, and Grades 7 and 12 where hot or reducing chemistry rules out Grade 2.

Types of plate and finishes

Hot rolled and annealed

The standard condition for plate: rolled hot to thickness, then annealed so the metal is soft, uniform and formable. The properties tabled on this page are the minimums for the annealed condition.

Descaled surface

Hot rolling leaves an oxide scale. Pickling removes it and leaves a uniform matt grey surface; ask for it when the plate will be welded or fabricated and surface condition matters.

Cut to size

Plate can be cut to your part sizes by waterjet, laser or band saw rather than bought as a full sheet. Cutting parameters for titanium differ from steel's, so give us the thickness and the part.

Advantages of titanium plate

Corrosion resistance
A thin oxide film forms on the surface and repairs itself wherever oxygen or moisture is present. It resists seawater, chlorides and oxidising acids that attack stainless steel.
Strength for its weight
Titanium's density is about 4.5 g/cm³ against roughly 8.0 for stainless steel — more than 40% lighter — and the alloy grades are as strong as many steels.
Weldable
Commercially pure grades fusion-weld readily, provided the weld and the metal beside it are shielded with inert gas on both sides while hot.
Low thermal expansion
Titanium expands about half as much as austenitic stainless steel when heated, which keeps joints and close-fitting parts stable through temperature swings.
Biocompatible
Titanium does not provoke the body's defences, which is why Grades 2, 5 and 23 are used for medical and dental components.

Uses of titanium plate

Heat exchangers
Tube sheets and shells for seawater and chloride-bearing duty, in Grade 2, with Grades 7 and 12 where conditions are harsher.
Chemical reactors and vessels
Pressure vessels and process columns where chlorides or oxidising acids would corrode stainless steel.
Desalination and offshore
Plate for seawater systems on platforms and desalination plant.
Aerospace structures
Machined structural parts in Grade 5, where strength per kilogram decides.
Electrochemical
Substrates for anodes in chlor-alkali and plating plant.
Power generation
Condenser tube sheets and water boxes on coastal and estuarine plant.

Titanium Plate — frequently asked

Which standard applies to titanium plate?
ASTM B265, which covers titanium and titanium alloy strip, sheet and plate; ASME SB-265 is the same specification for pressure-vessel work. Round bar is covered by ASTM B348 instead. Tell us which you buy to and we confirm what we can supply against it.
Which grade of titanium plate should I choose?
Grade 2 for general corrosion and seawater duty. Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) where strength per weight decides. Grade 7 or Grade 12 for hot reducing acids or hot brines, where Grade 2 would corrode. Grade 23 where an ELI alloy is required. Send the service conditions and we will advise.
How much does titanium plate weigh?
Commercially pure titanium weighs about 4.5 kg per square metre per millimetre of thickness — a 10 mm plate is about 45 kg/m². Grade 5 is slightly lighter at 4.43 g/cm³. The weight chart on this page lists common thicknesses; actual weight varies within the thickness tolerance.
Can titanium plate be welded?
Yes. Commercially pure grades fusion-weld readily, but titanium absorbs oxygen, nitrogen and hydrogen quickly at welding temperature, so the weld and the heat-affected zone must be shielded with inert gas on both sides. Contamination shows as a coloured oxide and embrittles the joint.
Do you cut titanium plate to size?
Yes — by waterjet, laser or band saw, subject to thickness. Tell us the part dimensions and quantity and we quote cut sizes rather than a full plate, which also cuts the cost of material you would otherwise pay for and discard.
Do you supply titanium pipe or tube?
No. We supply titanium as sheet, plate and round bar. If your requirement is pipe or tube we would rather tell you now than waste your time.
