What is round bar?

Titanium round bar — also called titanium rod — is solid cylindrical titanium, hot finished, drawn or ground to diameter and cut to length. It is the starting material for machined parts: shafts, fasteners, valve components, fittings and medical components.

Round bar is specified to ASTM B348, which covers titanium and titanium alloy bars and billets; ASME SB-348 is its pressure-vessel form. Grade 2 is the corrosion-resistant commercially pure choice, Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) the strength grade for shafts and fasteners, and Grade 23 the ELI alloy for demanding medical and aerospace parts.

Advantages of titanium round bar

Strength for its weight
Titanium's density is about 4.5 g/cm³ against roughly 8.0 for stainless steel — more than 40% lighter — and the alloy grades are as strong as many steels.
Corrosion resistance
A thin oxide film forms on the surface and repairs itself wherever oxygen or moisture is present. It resists seawater, chlorides and oxidising acids that attack stainless steel.
Biocompatible
Titanium does not provoke the body's defences, which is why Grades 2, 5 and 23 are used for medical and dental components.
Non-magnetic
Titanium is not attracted to a magnet, which matters in electronics, instrumentation and medical imaging environments.
Weldable
Commercially pure grades fusion-weld readily, provided the weld and the metal beside it are shielded with inert gas on both sides while hot.

Uses of titanium round bar

Fasteners and fixings
Bolts, studs and nuts machined from Grade 5 and Grade 2 bar, where corrosion or weight rules out steel.
Shafts and pump parts
Shafts, stems and impellers in seawater and chemical pumps.
Valves and fittings
Valve bodies, balls, stems and fittings machined from bar for aggressive process fluids.
Medical and dental

Aerospace
Machined structural and engine parts in Grade 5.
Motorsport
Connecting rods, valves and fasteners, where reducing weight in moving parts pays directly.

Titanium Round Bar — frequently asked

Which standard applies to titanium round bar?
ASTM B348, which covers titanium and titanium alloy bars and billets; ASME SB-348 is the same specification for pressure-vessel work. Sheet and plate fall under ASTM B265 instead. Tell us which you buy to and we confirm what we can supply against it.
What is the difference between titanium Grade 2 and Grade 5 round bar?
Grade 2 is commercially pure titanium: excellent corrosion resistance, easy to weld, minimum tensile strength 345 MPa. Grade 5 is the Ti-6Al-4V alloy: about two and a half times as strong, at 895 MPa minimum, but harder to machine and weld. Choose Grade 2 for corrosion, Grade 5 for strength.
How much does titanium round bar weigh?
Weight per metre is density times cross-sectional area. In commercially pure titanium a 20 mm bar weighs about 1.42 kg per metre and a 50 mm bar about 8.86 kg per metre; Grade 5 is slightly lighter. The weight chart on this page lists common diameters.
Is titanium round bar the same as titanium rod?
In practice, yes — the two names are used interchangeably, and both are covered by ASTM B348. Buyers tend to say 'rod' for smaller diameters and 'bar' for larger ones, but there is no fixed line, so quote the diameter and length you need.
Can I buy titanium bar for medical implants?
Surgical implants are specified to ASTM F136 (Grade 23 ELI), which is a different standard from B348 with tighter limits and its own testing. Tell us the standard your part is made to and we confirm what we can supply against it before you order.
Do you supply titanium pipe or tube?
No. We supply titanium as sheet, plate and round bar. If your requirement is pipe or tube we would rather tell you now than waste your time.
