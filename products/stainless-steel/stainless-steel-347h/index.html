Stainless Steel 347H

Stainless Steel 347H (UNS S34709) to ASTM A240 from Ramani Steel House, importer and stockholder in Mumbai since 1974. Request current sizes, stock and pricing.

Stainless Steel 347H is a niobium-stabilised austenitic stainless steel, UNS S34709. It is supplied to ASTM A240 in plate, sheet and strip. The standard sets a minimum tensile strength of 515 MPa and a minimum yield strength of 205 MPa in the annealed condition. Ramani Steel House supplies it from Mumbai, India — from stock and against order, cut to size, with the mill test certificate for the heat supplied.

An austenitic stainless stabilised with niobium, with carbon held to 0.04–0.10% for high-temperature strength. Niobium carbides stay stable to higher temperatures than titanium carbides, which makes 347H the grade for long, continuous high-temperature service.
