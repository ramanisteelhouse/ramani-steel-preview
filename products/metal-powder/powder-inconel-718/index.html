Inconel 718 Metal Powder is a precipitation-hardening nickel superalloy powder, UNS N07718. It is supplied to ASTM F3055 in binder jetting, fine mim, laser powder bed fusion and directed energy deposition, thermal spray. Ramani Steel House supplies it from Mumbai, India — from stock and against order, cut to size, with the mill test certificate for the heat supplied.

Precipitation-hardening nickel superalloy powder, and the most printed superalloy. Holds strength and creep resistance at high temperature while remaining weldable enough to print reliably.

Inconel 718 Metal Powder — frequently asked

Which particle size cut of INCONEL-718 do I need?
The micron cut, not the chemistry, decides which machine can run the powder. Binder jetting and fine MIM take the finest cut; laser powder bed fusion runs the mid range; directed energy deposition and electron beam melting need coarser, more flowable powder that will feed through a nozzle or resist scatter under the beam. Tell us your machine and process and we will match the cut.
Is a Certificate of Analysis supplied?
Yes, batch-wise with every lot. Powder is referenced to its ASTM or DIN designation, and the certificate reports the chemistry for the batch supplied.
