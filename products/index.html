Product range

Nickel alloys, stainless steel, titanium and busbars

Grades are listed exactly as we supply them, with UNS designations where they apply. Availability changes with the market, so send your requirement and we will confirm current stock, form and size.

Supplied in sheets, plates, coil, round bars, flat bars, pipes, tubes, wires, precision strip coils.

Pure Nickel

Commercially pure nickel in grades 200, 201, N6 and 270, held in Mumbai stock for chemical, chlor-alkali, electrical and battery applications.

Nickel Alloys & Super Alloys

Nickel-based alloys and super alloys for high-temperature and corrosive service, covering the Inconel, Incoloy, Monel, Hastelloy and Nimonic families.

Titanium & Titanium Alloys

Commercially pure titanium and titanium alloys from Grade 1 through Grade 23, for aerospace, chemical processing and medical applications.

Stainless Steel

Austenitic stainless steel in the 300 series, including the low-carbon, high-carbon and stabilised grades used across process and construction industries.

Duplex & Super Duplex

Duplex and super duplex stainless steel for chloride-bearing and high-strength service, together with the precipitation-hardening grades.

6Mo Stainless Steel

Super-austenitic 6-molybdenum stainless steel for seawater, desalination and high-chloride duty where 316L is not sufficient.

Special Stainless Steel

Heat-resistant and specialised corrosion-resistant stainless grades for furnace, kiln and aggressive chemical environments.

Busbars & Nickel Strip

Nickel strip punched to your cell layout, plus copper, aluminium, nickel-plated and clad busbars for lithium-ion packs in EV, solar and energy storage.
