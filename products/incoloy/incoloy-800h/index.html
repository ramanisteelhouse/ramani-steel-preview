Incoloy 800H

Incoloy 800H (UNS N08810) to ASTM B409 from Ramani Steel House, importer and stockholder in Mumbai since 1974. Request current sizes, stock and pricing.

Incoloy® is a registered trademark of Special Metals Corporation. Ramani Steel House is an independent stockholder and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the trademark owner. The grade name identifies the material specification supplied.

Incoloy 800H is an iron-nickel-chromium alloy, UNS N08810 / Werkstoff 1.4958. It is supplied to ASTM B409 in round bar, plate and sheet. The standard sets a minimum tensile strength of 448 MPa and a minimum yield strength of 172 MPa in the solution annealed condition. Ramani Steel House supplies it from Mumbai, India — from stock and against order, cut to size, with the mill test certificate for the heat supplied.

Incoloy 800 with carbon held to 0.05–0.10% and a controlled coarse grain size. Those two controls give it much better creep and rupture strength than standard 800, which is why it is specified for load-bearing service at high temperature.

Incoloy 800H — frequently asked

What is the difference between Incoloy 800, 800H and 800HT?
Same base chemistry, different controls. 800 is the general grade. 800H restricts carbon to 0.05–0.10% and requires a coarse grain size for creep strength. 800HT restricts carbon further and adds a combined aluminium plus titanium range, giving the highest creep-rupture strength of the three. For long-term load at high temperature, specify 800H or 800HT rather than 800.
